Antisuppression Bars puts red-green (or red-blue) bars over reading text and simple games, for
binocular vision exercises at home with bichrome glasses. Each eye sees only part of the picture
through its filter, so both eyes have to work together to read the story or follow the game.

<div class="notice">
<strong>Important:</strong> this is a supplement to vision therapy prescribed by an optometrist or
ophthalmologist. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
</div>

<a href="{{ site.play_url | relative_url }}"><strong>▶ Play in your browser</strong></a>. Works on
computers, tablets and phones, with no sign-up or download.

## Before you start

1. Turn off night mode, blue-light or eye-comfort filters on your screen. They change the colours.
2. Put on your red-green or red-blue glasses.
3. Open **Settings** and set **Glasses** to match them.
4. Adjust the **red** and **green / blue** shades until each eye sees only its own colour of bar.
   Screens show colour differently, so the best shade depends on your display.
5. Follow the session length and schedule your eye-care provider recommends.

## Settings

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Glasses | red / green, red / blue |
| Orientation | horizontal or vertical bars |
| Bar width | 20 to 200 px |
| Bar strength | 20% to 100% |
| Red shade | 8 shades |
| Green shade or Blue shade | 9 shades (matches the chosen glasses) |
| Text size | 32 to 72 px, for the reading stories |

Settings and best scores are saved in your browser.
