**In short:** Fusion Lab does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything
it remembers stays on your device.

## What the app stores

To remember your preferences, the app saves a small amount of information in local storage on
your device (your browser, or your TV's app storage):

- Your bar settings: glasses type, orientation, bar width, bar strength and colour shades
- Your text size for the reading screen
- Whether you have seen the first-run notice
- Your best score in each game

This information never leaves your device. It is not linked to your name, account or any other
identifier.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information, health information, contacts or location.
- It does not use the camera or microphone.
- It does not send data to any server, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Deleting your data

On LG TV, uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. In a web browser, clearing this
site's data (cookies and site data) removes it.

## Children

The app is suitable for children using it with a parent or eye-care professional. Because it
collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

## Medical notice

Fusion Lab is a supplement to vision therapy prescribed by an optometrist or
ophthalmologist. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## Third-party services

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP
addresses under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
On LG TVs, downloading and running apps is handled by LG's platform and LG Content Store, which
have their own privacy policies. This policy covers only the Fusion Lab app.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
For questions, open an issue on the [project's GitHub page](https://github.com/Zouhairzed/AntisuppressionBars/issues)
or use the developer contact listed on the app's page in LG Content Store.
