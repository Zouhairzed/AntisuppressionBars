      Before you start

      This app is a supplement to vision therapy prescribed by an optometrist or ophthalmologist. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

      Turn off any screen colour filters (night mode, blue-light filters), then wear your red-green or red-blue glasses and choose the matching type in Settings.
